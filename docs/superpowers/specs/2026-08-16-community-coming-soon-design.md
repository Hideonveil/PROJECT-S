# Community Coming Soon Design

## Scope

Only change the expanded public community surface. Do not change its opening animation, dimensions, close control, background, route, or behavior.

## Content and Hierarchy

- Keep `社区` as the dominant display word.
- Add `COMING SOON` as a small uppercase status label near the lower-right edge of the `社区` word group.
- Use the existing mono utility type, wide tracking, and subdued dark ink.
- The label is plain text: no button behavior, link, card, icon, animation, or fake feature.

## Responsive and Accessibility

- Keep the label legible without overlapping `社区` or the close control.
- At narrow widths, allow the label to sit below the main word.
- The visible text must be available to assistive technology.

## Verification

- Opening community shows both `社区` and `COMING SOON`.
- Closing community still restores the homepage.
- Existing community surface animation and dimensions remain unchanged.
